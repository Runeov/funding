What KrobJob can offer today

KrobJob can provide the room assessment, sensing-node setup, local processing, calibration workflow and owner-facing interface. The pilot proves performance in the real room before any operational use.

  • Defined resting or seated sensing zone
  • Reference-device comparison during calibration
  • Local signal processing and visible consent controls
  • Estimated pulse rhythm shown only when signal quality is sufficient

Important boundary

This is an experimental wellness and safety signal—not an ECG, diagnosis, emergency alarm or medical device. Movement, multiple people, room layout and node placement can reduce reliability. Every deployment requires explicit consent, local validation and legal review.
